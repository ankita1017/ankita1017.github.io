Product Manager, Builder.

Drawn to interesting problems, people, and patterns, and to making complex things easier to understand.

I am a Staff Product Manager at Clari, building the data platform that powers its AI applications.

For the past nine years, my work has centered on data and building products around it. I still love getting into its messy parts - finding errors and anomalies, spotting patterns, and sharpening my intuition about when to use it, question it, or ignore it.

That instinct carries into the products I build. I have worked across early-stage startups and scaling enterprise companies, taking products from their first versions to systems that need to work reliably at scale. I like working close to the technical details where product, data, and engineering come together. I am happiest with ambiguous, interesting problems that need systems thinking and close work with customers. Beyond work, I love making data art and writing about things I am curious about.
